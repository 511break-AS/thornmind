For iPhone 4S
on iOS 6.1.3

Siri4gain has only been tested on an iPhone 4S running iOS 6.1.3. Installing it on other devices or iOS versions is not recommended.

Siri4gain

The original iOS 6 Siri and dictation, back to life and fully offline.

What it does

Apple's iOS 6 Siri servers are gone. Siri4gain runs a small Siri server on the iPhone itself, so the stock Siri and the keyboard dictation key work again, with no internet connection.

Speech recognition runs on the phone with OpenAI Whisper tiny, in the 8 original Siri languages: English, Italian, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese and Korean.

Siri tells the time and date, also in other cities, sets timers and alarms, opens apps and understands follow-ups like "And in Tokyo?". The full command list is in Settings > Siri4gain.

On-device AI (optional)

Siri can answer other questions with SmolLM2-135M, a small language model that runs on the phone. It is off by default: turn on LLM Answers in Settings > Siri4gain. Its answers may be inaccurate or made up, and it only speaks English.

Notes

Needs about 175 MB of free space. Turn Siri on in Settings > General > Siri after installing. No certificates or hosts file changes are needed.
