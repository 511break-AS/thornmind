This notice explains how we process the personal data of people who visit thornmind.it or write to info@thornmind.it, in accordance with Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003).

01 Data controller

Thornmind is a non-commercial research project. The data controller is the person who runs it:

Alberto Sanfelice
Email: info@thornmind.it
Certified email (PEC): alberto.sanfelice@pec.it

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed, as one is not required for the processing described in this notice.

02 What data we process

Browsing data

When you visit the site, the server that hosts it (GitHub) automatically receives some technical data, as happens with any website: IP address, browser and operating system type, page requested, date and time of the visit, and referring page. This data is needed to run the site and protect it from abuse. Thornmind has no access to it and does not use it to identify visitors.

Data you send us by email

If you write to us, we process your email address, your name and any information you choose to include in your message. Please do not send us special categories of data (such as health data) unless strictly necessary.

Language preference

If you choose a language with the IT/EN switch, your browser remembers that choice. See Cookies and similar technologies for details.

03 Purposes and legal bases

  • Replying to your messages and handling dataset or collaboration proposals. Legal basis: your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying to you (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Running the site and keeping it secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Complying with legal obligations or defending legal claims, where necessary. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing or profiling.

04 Who receives the data

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States), which hosts the site through GitHub Pages and processes browsing data under its own privacy statement.
  • Register.it S.p.A. (Italy), which provides the email service for info@thornmind.it, acting as data processor.
  • Public authorities, only where required by law.

Your data is never sold, handed over or published.

05 Transfers outside the European Union

GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States. Browsing data is transferred on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, in which GitHub participates, and of the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission. Email data stays with Register.it, in Italy.

06 How long we keep it

  • Emails: for as long as needed to handle your request, and then for 12 months after the conversation ends. If a collaboration starts, we keep them for as long as it lasts. After these periods we delete them, unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Browsing data: for the periods set by GitHub in its privacy statement.
  • Language preference: it stays in your browser until you delete it.

07 Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses no cookies, neither its own nor third-party ones. It uses no analytics tools, social media buttons, or embedded videos or maps. Fonts, images and animations are hosted on the site itself, so opening its pages sends no data to other services.

The only information stored on your device is the language you choose with the IT/EN switch. It is saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage) under the name tm-lang, with the value it or en, and only after you click the switch. It is never sent to us or anyone else and cannot be used to identify you.

This is a technical tool, needed to provide the service you asked for (remembering your language). It therefore does not require your consent (Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Italian Data Protection Authority's cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021). You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

08 Your rights

At any time you can ask us to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • correct or complete it (Art. 16);
  • erase it (Art. 17);
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18);
  • provide it in a machine-readable format (portability, Art. 20);
  • stop processing based on legitimate interest (objection, Art. 21).

To exercise these rights, write to info@thornmind.it or, if you prefer a channel with legal value, to our certified email address (PEC) alberto.sanfelice@pec.it. We will reply within one month.

If you believe your data is being processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work.

09 Other information

  • Voluntary: writing to us is entirely your choice. Without an email address, however, we cannot reply.
  • Automated decisions: we make no decisions based solely on automated processing and we do not profile anyone.
  • Children: the site is not aimed at children under 14. If you are under 14, please do not send us personal data without a parent's consent.
  • Datasets and research: this notice covers only the website and email contact. Data collection for datasets will be covered by specific notices, given to participants before each collection.

10 Visitors outside the European Union

We give all visitors, wherever they are, the protections provided by the GDPR. In particular, for United States residents: we do not sell or share personal data, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not collect sensitive data through the site. Because we do not track visitors, signals such as Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track are respected by default.

11 Changes to this notice

We may update this notice, for example if we add new features to the site. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of the page.
