Mission

An intelligence is only as good as the data it learns from.

Every model carries the choices made about its data. That is why Thornmind brings two sides together in a single project: developing artificial intelligence and collecting the datasets needed to train it. It is a research project with no commercial purpose.

We work so that every piece of data has a clear origin, explicit consent and a precise purpose, so that the models built on it are reliable, verifiable and useful.

What we do

From data collection to trained model.

Three areas of work that feed into each other.

Datasets

We collect, clean and annotate text, image and audio data, with datasheets that explain their origin, limitations and intended uses.

  • Collection and selection
  • Annotation and validation
  • Full documentation

Models

We design and train artificial intelligence for research, with no commercial purpose, evaluating it rigorously and documenting every result.

  • Architecture design
  • Training and fine-tuning
  • Evaluation and benchmarks

Open research

We publish methods, tools and results, and collaborate on a non-profit basis with anyone who wants to contribute data, skills or ideas.

  • Shared tools
  • Collaborations
  • Publications
